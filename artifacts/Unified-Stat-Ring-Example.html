Single shared ring, not six separate trees: every primary and seam node is a stat pick, and investing further in any of them unlocks the ability that complements it. A Warrior who keeps walking toward Ranger (the AGI seam) ends up with Probing Thrust, then Vanishing Cut deep enough in — a Thief-flavored physical build, built entirely from Warrior's own existing Shadowstep kit. A Warrior who instead walks toward Cleric (the WIS seam) reaches Lay on Hands, an early Paladin-style heal that doesn't exist in content yet. Ring order (answers GG-T83): Ranger–Warrior–Cleric–Guardian–Alchemist–Mage, chosen so every seam's two neighbors already share real sub-archetype content wherever that content exists today.
